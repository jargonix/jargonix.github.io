OLM Viewer: Reader & Converter

OLM Viewer & Converter is a powerful, privacy-first utility designed to help you easily open OLM files, read OLM email archives, and export mailbox data natively on your device.

WHETHER YOU NEED AN OLM READER OR CONVERTER:
If you have a Mac Outlook file (file OLM) and need an efficient OLM viewer or OLM reader, this app provides an intuitive solution to inspect and process your data. You can open OLM files instantly without needing third-party software or uploading sensitive mailboxes to external cloud servers.

KEY FEATURES:
• Seamless OLM File Viewing: Open OLM archives, browse nested mail folders, read OLM email messages, view calendar entries, and check contacts.
• Convert OLM File to CSV: Export structured mailbox records to spreadsheets. Use our OLM to CSV converter capabilities to convert OLM to CSV on Mac or iOS, or convert OLM file to Excel on Mac for easy data analysis.
• Convert OLM to MBOX: Need an OLM to MBOX converter? Export your email folders into universal MBOX formats (convert OLM to MBOX Mac) for seamless mail client migration.
• Convert OLM to PDF & EML: Easily convert OLM to PDF for printing and document archiving, or export messages as standard EML files.
• Export Contacts & Calendars: Convert OLM to ICS for calendar events and vCard for address book contacts.

MAILBOX MIGRATION & EXPORT OPTIONS:
If you are searching for an OLM to PST converter or need to convert OLM file to PST format, our tool provides the ideal intermediate formats. While direct OLM to PST conversion is restricted on mobile, exporting your file OLM to MBOX or CSV enables smooth workflows to import OLM to Mac Mail, import OLM to Gmail, import OLM to Google Calendar, or import OLM to Office 365.

Users looking to import OLM file data into enterprise platforms can use our generated standard files to import OLM to Office 365 Mac or import OLM into Office 365 accounts smoothly. Whether you need a Mac OLM converter, an OLM converter for Mac desktop/mobile environments, or a trusted OLM to CSV converter for Mac, this tool handles your archive export requirements effortlessly.

PRIVACY & SECURITY FIRST:
• 100% On-Device Processing: Every OLM file is parsed locally. Your emails, attachments, and personal records never leave your physical device.
• Tracking Prevention: External tracking pixels and remote images are blocked by default to protect your privacy.
